Hi. This is the third time I've rewritten my portfolio. To be honest, I'm tired of all the Next.js ecosystem slop.

This new website uses [Zola](https://getzola.dev), a static site generator. The [theme](https://github.com/spxnso/umbra) I used is made by me and is open-source, feel free to check it out.

## Why Zola?

I've tried Next.js, and even a brief (regrettable) stint with a headless CMS. To be honest, I was tired of updating all the dependencies every week thanks to the awesome Next.js exploits (HM: react2shell).

Zola was the answer to my problem. It's a single binary, written in Rust, that takes markdown files and spits out HTML. No npm, no slop. It just works, and it's fast.
